# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A Simple Interest Calculator calculates the interest earned or payable on a principal amount over a specified period at a given annual interest rate.

## Formula
Simple Interest (SI) = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- **Principal (P):** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate (R):** The annual interest rate in percentage.
- **Time (T):** The duration in years.

## Example
- Principal: ₹10,000
- Annual Interest Rate: 5%
- Time: 2 years

Calculation:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000

**Simple Interest:** ₹1,000

**Total Amount:** ₹11,000

## Features
- Calculates simple interest using the standard formula.
- Accepts principal, annual interest rate, and time.
- Displays the simple interest and total amount.

## Technologies
- Markdown (README documentation)

## How to Use
1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the time period in years.
4. Apply the simple interest formula to calculate the interest.
5. Add the interest to the principal to obtain the total amount.
